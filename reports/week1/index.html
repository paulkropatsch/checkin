How it's counted
Method notes

Headline: total drinks logged Tue–Mon by people who checked in all 7 days and weren't exempt, divided by the number of those people (n = 5). Per-day bars average the same people. People with missed days aren't in the drink averages but still count toward check-in rates.

Everything here is group-level. No individual results are shown.
